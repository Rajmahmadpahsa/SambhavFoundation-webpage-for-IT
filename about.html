CENTRAL TRAINING TEAM

THE BACKBONE OF TRAINING QUALITY

At Sambhav Foundation, the Central Training Team functions as the quality and capability anchor for all training operations across India. While training delivery happens at centres and through partners, our role is to ensure that every learner, irrespective of geography or trade, receives a consistent, relevant, and employment-oriented learning experience.

"Training delivery may be decentralised. Training quality is not."
